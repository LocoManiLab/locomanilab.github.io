What is
LocoManiLab?

A full-stack testbed for humanoid loco-manipulation.

Bring tasks, data, policies, and whole-body control into one workflow—from simulation to real-world deployment.

THE FULL STACK, IN MOTION

01 / 07 TASKS & ENVIRONMENTS

Start with everyday tasks.

Open a fridge, crouch to reach a low drawer, pull out a chair, lift and carry objects, or pack them into a basket. Compose these whole-body skills into longer household tasks.

  • 102 atomic tasks
  • 132 compositional tasks
  • 13 scenes
Explore the scenes ↗

02 / 07 DATA COLLECTION & GENERATION

Demonstrate. Generate. Learn.

Capture whole-body demonstrations through VR teleoperation in simulation and on real robots, and expand training data with motion synthesis. Keep vision, language, actions, and state synchronized.

  • VR teleoperation (sim & real)
  • Motion generation
  • Policy-ready data
Explore the data ↗

03 / 07 POLICIES

Bring your policy.

Bring together robot state, language instructions, and images to train and compare vision-language-action and world-action policies, with a shared embodiment and policy-specific data adapters.

  • 12 integrated policies
  • VLA · WAM
  • State · Instructions · Images
Meet the policies ↗

04 / 07 CONTROLLERS

Connect decisions to motion.

Pair policies with five whole-body controllers, each with its own action and state interface, on the dataset’s Unitree G1 with Dex3 dexterous hands.

  • Locomotion
  • Manipulation
  • Whole-body coordination

More humanoids to come.

Compare the controllers ↗

05 / 07 DOMAIN RANDOMIZATION

One task. Many variations.

Vary appearance and lighting, add distracting objects, and change target positions and orientations. Test the same skill under different visual and spatial conditions.

  • Appearance & lighting
  • Distractors
  • Target poses
Watch appearance randomization ↗

06 / 07 TASK & MOTION EVALUATION

Evaluate behavior at every level.

Compare task success and generalization alongside stability, motion quality, and efficiency—across tasks, scenes, and randomization settings. Explore demonstration scaling and open-space motion transfer in the experimental studies.

  • Task success
  • Motion quality
  • Generalization · Scaling
Explore Loco-Mani Arena ↗

07 / 07 SIM2REAL

Take policies into the real world.

Bridge simulation and the physical G1 with consistent task and deployment interfaces. Explore refrigerator opening, shelf-to-table transfer, and conveyor pick-and-place across simulation and real trials.

  • Real-to-sim scenes
  • Sim-to-real transfer
  • Unitree G1
